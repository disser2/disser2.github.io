How it works:

Colors: Light gray (0), Yellow (1), Orange (2), Pink (3), Red (4+)

Toppling Rule: When a cell reaches 4 or more grains, it topples and gives 1 grain to each of its 4 neighbors.

Interactions: Click any cell to add 1 grain. Use "Start Adding" to continuously add random grains.
